UofT St George Campus Highlights

Hart House

Hart House is a student-focused centre for the arts, dialogue and wellness at the University of Toronto.

Details
Since it first opened its doors in 1919, Hart House has sought to function as a place where students—as well as faculty, staff, alumni and members of the broader community—find welcome and unique ways to connect with each other and the broader world through engagement with the arts, dialogue and wellness.
Hart House

Commissioned in 1911 by Vincent Massey, Hart House was completed in 1919 and gifted to the University of Toronto as a gathering place for students.

Robarts Library

Robarts Library is Canada's largest academic library and the hub for humanities and social sciences at U of T.

Details
With extensive print and digital collections, it also offers diverse study and collaboration spaces, including group study rooms, the Robarts Common, the Reflection Room, the Family Study Space, and the Digital Studio.
Robarts Library

For the U of T community, Robarts has always been more than an iconic building—its the symbolic heart of the university, bringing together everyone from every discipline to help make lasting change around the world.

Rotman School of Management

The Joseph L. Rotman School of Management is the graduate business school of the University of Toronto.

Details
The Rotman School is part of the University of Toronto, a global centre of research and academic excellence at the heart of Canadas multicultural commercial capital.
Rotman School of Management

Rotman is a catalyst for transformative learning, insights and public engagement, bringing together diverse views and initiatives around a defining purpose: to create value for business and society.
OISE Building

The Ontario Institute for Studies in Education serving 3,300 graduate students.

Details
OISE offers graduate programs in teaching, psychology, educational theory, history and administration, and Honours Specialist, technical education diploma, and Additional and Principal Qualification programs.
OISE

OISE traces its origins back to three separate institutions the Ontario Provincial Normal School, the Faculty of Education at the University of Toronto, and the Ontario Institute for Studies in Education.

The Daniels Building

The Daniels Building is an academic building in Toronto that houses the John H. Daniels Faculty of Architecture, Landscape, and Design

Details
The building offers space for design studios, lounges, a student cafe, workshops and fabrication labs. The historic building was sensitively restored and renovated to accommodate the new library, classes and faculty offices.
The Daniels Building

The extensive project received several awards for architectural excellence and was published in magazines around the world.
Jackman Law Building

The Henry N.R. Jackman Faculty of Law is the law school of the University of Toronto.

Details
Jackman Law Building was completed in 2016, with an award-winning design by Hariri Pontarini Architects.
Jackman Law Building

Its soaring Osler, Hoskin & Harcourt LLP Atrium is the central meeting point of the law school.
